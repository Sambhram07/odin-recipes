Chicken Ghee Roast

Description

This is a famous Mangalorean delicacy known for its rich, spicy, and tangy flavor profile. The chicken is cooked to perfection in pure ghee with a freshly roasted spice paste.

Ingredients

  • Chicken: 500g (bone-in or boneless)
  • Ghee (Clarified Butter): 4-5 tablespoons
  • Yogurt & Lemon Juice: For marination
  • Dry Red Chilies: 6-8 (Byadgi chilies work best for color)
  • Whole Spices: Coriander, cumin, fennel, black pepper, and fenugreek seeds
  • Garlic & Tamarind Paste: For the signature spice blend

Steps for Preparing

  1. Marinate chicken with yogurt, turmeric, lemon juice, and salt for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Dry roast whole spices and red chilies, then blend them with garlic and tamarind into a smooth paste.
  3. Heat 2 tablespoons of ghee in a pan and sear the marinated chicken until partially cooked, then set aside.
  4. In the same pan, roast the prepared spice paste in remaining ghee until it turns aromatic and releases oil.
  5. Add the cooked chicken to the masala, toss well to coat, and simmer on low heat until juicy and fully cooked.
