Mojito

Description

This is a classic Cuban cocktail that is crisp, refreshing, and full of citrus and mint flavors. It is perfect for hot summer days or relaxing with friends.

Ingredients

  • Fresh Mint Leaves: 6-8 leaves
  • Fresh Lime: 1/2 lime (cut into wedges)
  • Sugar or Simple Syrup: 2 teaspoons
  • White Rum: 60 ml
  • Club Soda or Sparkling Water: To top
  • Ice Cubes: Crushed ice or ice cubes

Steps for Preparing

  1. Muddle the mint leaves, lime wedges, and sugar in a glass to release the juices and oils.
  2. Fill the glass almost to the top with crushed ice.
  3. Pour the white rum over the ice.
  4. Top off the glass with fresh club soda and stir gently to combine all ingredients.
  5. Garnish with a sprig of fresh mint and a lime wheel before serving cold.
