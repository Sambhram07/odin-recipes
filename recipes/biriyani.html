Biriyani

Discription

This is my favourite dish. I fell in love with this dish when I was 9th standard.This is a famous dish when it comes to non veg.

Ingredients

  • Chicken: 1 kg (bone-in pieces work best for flavor)
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste: 2 tablespoons
  • Cooking Oil or Ghee: 2 tablespoons
  • Basmati Rice: 3 cups
  • Water: For boiling
  • Biryani Masala and Garam masala

Steps for Preparing

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, and spices for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Soak basmati rice for 30 minutes and boil it in whole-spice water until 70–80% cooked.
  3. Cook the marinated chicken partially in a heavy pot until the raw color disappears.
  4. Layer the parboiled rice evenly over the chicken, topping with fried onions, mint, ghee, and saffron milk.
  5. Seal the pot tightly and steam on low heat (dum) for 20 minutes before serving.
